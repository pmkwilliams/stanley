# Lucy Dacus
## Thu, Oct 22 · The Fillmore, San Francisco
Doors 7:00 PM · Show 8:00 PM · All ages
[Buy tickets]
## About this event
Lucy Dacus brings her Forever Is a Feeling tour to The Fillmore for one night only, with support from Jay Som.
Tickets are general admission, standing room only. A limited number of balcony seats are available on a first-come, first-served basis.
## Venue information
The Fillmore is at 1805 Geary Blvd. The nearest BART station is Civic Center; Muni lines 38 and 22 stop outside.
Bags larger than 12 x 12 x 6 inches are not allowed. There is no coat check.
